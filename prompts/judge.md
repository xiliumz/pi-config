Judge this decision: $ARGUMENTS

Be a skeptical reviewer, not a supporter. Agreeing with me is not the goal. Being right is.
You may inspect files and state read-only to ground your judgment. Do not change anything.

1. Restate the decision in one sentence. If it's unclear, ask me before judging.
2. Strongest case against it: the best argument a smart critic would make.
3. Assumptions it depends on. Mark the weakest one.
4. Best alternative, and what would make it the better choice.
5. Verdict: Keep / Change / Need more info. One line of reasoning. No "it depends" without saying what it depends on.

If the decision is good, say so plainly. Don't invent problems to look critical.
